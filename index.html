Frequently asked questions

Do you sell Machu Picchu tickets?

No. We do not sell Machu Picchu entry tickets, train tickets or bus tickets. We help you review your logistics and prepare clear questions for your agency or official providers.

Are transfer prices guaranteed?

No. All prices shown or referenced are indicative and must be confirmed by a local transport provider. Final rates depend on vehicle type, route conditions, fuel prices and demand.

Is this an official transport company?

No. Cusco Travel Helper is an independent trip assistance tool. We are not a transport operator, travel agency or government entity. We help you structure your route request and connect with local providers.

Can I book directly here?

You cannot complete a confirmed booking on this site. You can generate a detailed WhatsApp request and continue the conversation directly with a provider. Transport is confirmed only after a provider accepts the request.

What happens after I send the WhatsApp request?

A local provider will review your trip details and respond with availability, final price and vehicle information. We recommend confirming vehicle type, driver license, insurance and pickup protocol before agreeing.

Can you help if I already booked a tour package?

Yes. Our Trip Check services help you review your itinerary, identify missing logistics and prepare questions for your tour operator. We do not replace your agency, we add clarity.

Should I sleep in Cusco or Ollantaytambo before Machu Picchu?

It depends on your train schedule. Ollantaytambo is closer to Machu Picchu and useful for early trains. Cusco offers more accommodation and dining options. We can review your specific itinerary to recommend the best option.

Can I request a private driver?

Yes. You can specify your preference in the transfer form. Select your vehicle type and mention any specific needs in the additional notes. The provider will confirm driver availability.

Do you work with verified operators?

We are currently validating local providers. Always confirm vehicle, driver, license, insurance and final price before booking. We do not guarantee the quality of any specific operator.
